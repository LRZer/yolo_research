## Baseline and implementation audit

The first stage investigated whether dense one-to-many (O2M) supervision could improve a deployment one-to-one (O2O) head without adding inference work. The baseline was scratch YOLO26n, locked to Ultralytics 8.4.146 and research checkout `9d45ee4613e79d7bceef7a39e42c15c278b0d4e8`. VOC2007+2012 trainval contained 16,551 images / 40,058 retained boxes; VOC2007 test contained 4,952 images / 12,032 boxes. `difficult` targets were excluded under the common label conversion.

The 150-epoch seed 42 baseline used batch 64, 640 input, four RTX3090s and a fixed training schedule. Its historical training validator reported **O2M+NMS**: P=0.79629, R=0.71899, AP50=0.79339, AP95=0.58705. Independent evaluation of the same last checkpoint's **O2O NMS-free** path gave P=0.7910313, R=0.69714, AP50=0.7786028, AP95=0.5768901. The 0.58705 value must not be called the O2O baseline.

The code audit established three important facts: the O2O head consumes detached shared features; dense O2M supervision ordinarily updates the trunk while O2O supervision does not; this version uses `reg_max=1` direct regression, not the usual DFL configuration. The native progressive loss weights change from O2M/O2O=0.8/0.2 toward 0.1/0.9. Method design and interpretation were based on the inspected implementation, not generic descriptions of other YOLO releases.

D0 added diagnostics with transfer gain zero. Its 150-epoch weights were tensor-identical to B0. O2O AP95=0.57689 and O2M AP95≈0.58707 confirmed that instrumentation alone did not change this baseline. The [original baseline CSV](../../archives/local/yolo26n_voc_baseline_e150/results.csv), [research log](../../archives/local/yolo26_research/RESEARCH_LOG.md) and [D0 report](../../archives/local/yolo26_research/D0_DIAGNOSTIC_REPORT.md) preserve the exact values and validation contexts.

## Quality-gated knowledge transfer

Matched anchors were required to correspond to the same GT. Teacher reliability incorporated classification probability and localization quality, with a representative quality score $q=\sqrt{p_{gt}\,IoU}$. The initial D1/D2 comparison tested ungated versus relative-quality-gated classification transfer. The supervised soft-target BCE logit gradient is $p_s-y$; transfer toward a teacher probability has gradient $p_s-p_t$. A teacher can have a better box/score and still move the student **away from the native target**. This motivated direct gradient-direction probes rather than treating “teacher better” as sufficient.

A separate 50-epoch B50 was used for fair short-budget comparisons. It is not equivalent to stopping the 150-epoch schedule at epoch 50. Main fixed-epoch CSV results:

The original D2 gain was not a clean quality-gating effect. Normalizing by selected anchors meant that a sparse active set (~18%) produced a larger auxiliary gradient; same-gain D2 was 32–53% larger than D1 in the calibration panel. Global norm clipping coupled auxiliary and native updates: all 16 tested batches exceeded clip norm10, and the shared-update ratio was about 0.9844 at epoch 50. D2-GM reduced gain to match scale and erased the apparent gain. S-QGAT retired transfer at epoch 40 but did not improve AP. [D2](../../archives/local/yolo26_research/D2_FINAL_COMPARISON.md), [gain matching](../../archives/local/yolo26_research/D2_GM_FINAL_COMPARISON.md), [causal audit](../../archives/local/yolo26_research/PROBE_CAUSAL_AUDIT.md).

TQGB separated direction, assignment identity, reliability and gradient compatibility. For the classification route, a projected teacher probability was restricted to the interval between $p_s$ and $y$, preventing a sign-conflicting BCE direction. T1 improved seed 42 but failed seed 0. Its two-seed paired mean change was +0.002275, sample SD≈0.00626; only one seed was positive. T2 was weaker than T1 at seed 42. Projection added +0.00472 at seed 42 but −0.00159 at seed 0. The route was **stopped**, not rescued by selecting the successful seed. [Causal closure](../../archives/local/yolo26_research/TQGB_CAUSAL_CLOSURE_2026-09-19.md).

Independent O2O reevaluations are archived separately from rounded training CSVs. For example, seed 42 B50/T1/T2 AP95=0.5129009/0.5193847/0.5165675. Their O2M sidecars were0.5282397/0.5334306/0.5334126. These are different heads, not additional independent training successes. Derived $H=2PR/(P+R)$ is not the evaluator's native maximum-F1 statistic; a retrospective F1 audit explicitly corrected this distinction.

## Reproduction controls and failure attribution

T0/no-route exactly matched B50 on 3,750 comparable non-time values. A same-boot seed 0 B50 repeat matched all 5,350 comparable epoch cells and all 708 tensors / 2,530,706 elements, ruling out numerical baseline drift as the cause of seed 0's negative result. Raw file SHA differences can still reflect serialization and timestamps; numeric equivalence and byte equivalence are different tests.

Several D2 starts failed before producing any epoch because of uncorrectable memory errors/SIGBUS. A workers 0/no-pinned-memory isolation attempt also failed, including a DDP Python rank and unrelated processes, contradicting a purely DataLoader/shared-memory explanation. Invalid starts remain in the archive but do not count as method failures. Later valid runs retain the **faulty-host exploratory** qualification even where no new error appeared in their execution window.

FCA scanned16,551 images at each of six BF16 checkpoints, with last FP32 sensitivity verification. O2O gaps were28/1/0/0/0/2; all were conflict-stage events, not extra GT extinction from final `topk2`. The O2M sidecar had169 pooled gaps (0.0703%). Native conflict resolution could introduce edges outside pre-conflict top-k:152 O2O and 138 O2M added edges, but no coverage gain. The audit used the original graph semantics and an explicitly frozen A1 amendment rather than silently rewriting assignment behavior. [Full FCA report](../../archives/local/yolo26_research/FCA_P0_FINAL_AUDIT_2026-09-19.md).

ASAC controlled only scale/translation strength. It used the same train-only panel throughout, detached inference measurement, reference stability, an EMA, bounded log-rate change and q∈[.25,1]. Coverage and RNG/model/buffer invariants were audited. Of657 clean GT,624 survived every view;33 excluded objects were smaller and nearer image boundaries. Therefore the probe-go applied to surviving targets, not all edge/tiny targets.

## ASAC training result

Phase1a compared a live A2 controller with **instrumented fixed-q A0**, not the old B50. A0 diverged numerically after epoch 1 because its infrastructure reset the loader; it was not misrepresented as an exactly unchanged official baseline.

| A0 fixed q=1 | 0.74356 | 0.64914 | 0.72061 | 0.51357 |

A2's q ranged0.489525–1, mean≈0.809132, and reached 1 at epoch 31. AP95 was lower by 0.00510; last 5/last 10 average differences were−0.004564/−0.004721. A2 won7 epochs, tied1 and lost42. Runtime1973.6 vs1973.95s did not create an efficiency gain. The predefined AP gate failed; additional seeds, matched schedule/shuffled-feedback arms,150-epoch and COCO extensions were **not run**. [Final report](../../archives/local/yolo26_research/ASAC_PHASE1A_SEED42_FINAL_2026-09-19/REPORT.md).
